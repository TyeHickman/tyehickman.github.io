Grimmoire began as the visual language for an Obsidian knowledge base and is becoming a small publishable design system. One shared token layer feeds two platform adapters: an installable Obsidian theme and a standalone web stylesheet.

The separation is intentional. Color, typography, spacing, and effects can remain recognizable across both surfaces, while Obsidian selectors and website components stay in their own platform layers. The result is reusable without forcing a website to imitate an editor interface.

This site consumes the generated web artifact and keeps its own navigation, collections, and composition rules in a separate stylesheet.
